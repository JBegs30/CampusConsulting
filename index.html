What is collected

The project collects timestamped location data during the 2-day participation period to analyze student movement and campus foot traffic.

To reduce unnecessary identification, your mobility file should use a participant ID rather than your name.

Privacy

Location histories can reveal sensitive information such as where you live, study, or spend time. Participation should be voluntary, and the signup flow should clearly explain who can access raw files, how long they are retained, and whether only aggregated results are shared with the client.

You should not submit names inside exported mobility files. Keep raffle contact information separate from mobility data when possible.
